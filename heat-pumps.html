Heat Pumps — Green Offers

A warmer home, a cleaner way to heat it

What is it?

A heat pump is an efficient, low-carbon way to heat your home and water, and can replace an ageing gas or oil boiler. Depending on where you live and your home, you may be eligible for government support towards the cost.

Why homeowners choose heat pumps

  • An efficient, lower-carbon alternative to a gas or oil boiler
  • You may qualify for government grant support (eligibility varies)
  • Provides heating and hot water for your home
  • Quotes from installers with the right accreditations, such as MCS
