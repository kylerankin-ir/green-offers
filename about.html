About us

One place to compare energy and go greener

Green Offers helps UK households compare energy and explore green home upgrades — with real people, not just algorithms.

Who we are

Green Offers is a trading name of Interactive Results Limited, a UK company that connects households with energy deals and home-improvement providers. We bring energy comparison and green home upgrades — solar, battery storage, heat pumps and EV charging — together in one simple place.

What we do

We're a comparison and lead-generation service. You tell us a few details about your home and energy, and one of our advisors calls you back to talk through your options and match you with suitable, accredited providers. We don't carry out installations ourselves — we help you find the right people who do.

How we work

  • Free and no-obligation. Comparing and getting quotes through us never costs you anything.
  • Real advisors. You speak to a person who explains things in plain English, with no pressure.
  • Vetted providers. We look for installers who hold recognised accreditations, such as MCS and TrustMark.
  • Your data, respected. We handle your information in line with UK data protection law — see our privacy policy.
