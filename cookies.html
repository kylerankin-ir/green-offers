How we use cookies

Cookie Policy

The cookies we use, what they do, and how you can control them.

Last updated 14 October 2024

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember your preferences, and let us understand how the site is used so we can improve it.

How we use cookies

We use cookies in the following broad categories:

Strictly necessary

Required for the site to function — for example, keeping the site secure and remembering your progress through the quote form. These can't be switched off in our systems.

Analytics

Help us understand how visitors use the site (for example, which pages are viewed) so we can improve it. This data is aggregated and used to measure and improve performance.

Advertising

We use Google Ads and related Google services to measure the performance of our adverts and to show relevant ads. These cookies may record that you visited our site and completed an enquiry, so we can measure advertising effectiveness. See Google's advertising policies for more.

Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works. For more on managing cookies, see aboutcookies.org.

More information

For how we handle personal data more generally, see our privacy policy. Questions? Email dataprotection@interactiveresults.co.uk.
