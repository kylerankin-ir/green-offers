Solar Panels — Green Offers

Cut your bills with solar panels

What is it?

Solar panels (solar PV) turn daylight into electricity you can use in your home, so you buy less from the grid. Any surplus you don't use can be exported, and you may be paid for it under the Smart Export Guarantee.

Why homeowners choose solar

  • Generate your own electricity and rely less on the grid
  • Get paid for surplus energy you export back
  • Pair with a battery to use your solar power after dark
  • Quotes from installers who hold the right accreditations, such as MCS
