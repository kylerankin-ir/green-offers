How it works

Compare, call, and start saving

Three simple steps between you and a better deal — we do the comparing and the legwork.

1

Share a few details

Your postcode, current supplier and rough monthly spend — plus any green upgrades you're curious about. It takes under a minute and you don't need a bill to hand.

2

We compare & call

An advisor calls to understand your home and talks you through the best energy deals we can find — and any solar, battery, heat pump or EV options you flagged.

3

Switch & save

Happy with what we find? We help you make the switch and arrange quotes for any upgrades from vetted installers. No obligation at any point.

Is it really free?

Yes. Comparing your energy and getting quotes through Green Offers is completely free, with no obligation to go ahead.

Do I need to own my home?

Not to compare or switch energy — anyone can do that. Green upgrades like solar or a heat pump are fixed to the property, so they need the owner's authorisation; if you rent, you'd need your landlord's agreement for those.

What happens to my details?

We use your details to prepare your comparison and match you with suitable providers, in line with UK data protection law. See our privacy policy for the full picture.
