Battery Storage — Green Offers

Store your energy, use it any time

What is it?

A home battery stores electricity — from your solar panels or bought cheaply overnight — so you can use it later, including in the evening peak when energy is most expensive.

Why homeowners choose battery storage

  • Use more of the energy you generate or buy cheaply
  • Cut reliance on the grid at expensive peak times
  • Back-up power potential during outages (depending on setup)
  • Works alongside solar or on its own
